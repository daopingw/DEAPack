# Contributing to This Open Handbook

This Handbook is meant to improve through scrutiny and use. Readers are
welcome to report an unclear explanation, challenge an assumption, supply a
counterexample, locate a defining source, propose a teaching case or figure,
or improve the Chinese translation. A useful contribution may be a carefully
stated question; it does not have to arrive as finished code.

The Handbook and DEAPack have different admission thresholds. The package can
document a source-qualified specialization without giving it a separate place
in the teaching route. A new Handbook section is reserved for a transferable
idea that changes the production, performance, organizational, temporal,
valuation, environmental, or inferential question. Different acronyms for
the same substantive mechanism are explained together.

Use the repository's
[contribution guide](https://github.com/daopingw/DEAPack/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
or open a structured
[model proposal](https://github.com/daopingw/DEAPack/issues/new?template=model-proposal.yml),
[editorial or translation suggestion](https://github.com/daopingw/DEAPack/issues/new?template=documentation-translation.yml),
or
[bug report](https://github.com/daopingw/DEAPack/issues/new?template=bug-report.yml).
Package code, formulas, computed results, figures, English prose, and Chinese
prose are reviewed as one scholarly record even though they are published in
different forms.

For Bilingual Handbook Preview 1, these routes currently accept issue-based
corrections and suggestions rather than contributed manuscript or translation
text. Substantive Handbook pull requests remain closed until a professionally
reviewed copyright-assignment instrument has been executed by every required
party. Opening an issue, commit, or pull request is not an assignment; see the
[Handbook contribution rights policy](https://github.com/daopingw/DEAPack/blob/main/book/HANDBOOK_CONTRIBUTION_POLICY.md).

Accepted contributions remain visible in version history and are credited in
the place that best preserves their provenance. Handbook authorship and DOI
metadata are separate editorial decisions and are not assigned automatically
by a pull request. Data, figures, and substantial third-party material must
also carry a clear source and redistribution license before publication.
