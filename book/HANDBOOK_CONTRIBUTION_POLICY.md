# Handbook Contribution Rights Policy

This file is a contribution boundary, not a copyright-assignment agreement
and not legal advice.

Bilingual Handbook Preview 1 is distributed as an All Rights Reserved
manuscript. The intended long-term route for substantive English manuscript
or Chinese translation contributions is a written copyright-assignment
arrangement that preserves contributor credit while allowing coordinated
editing, translation, publication, and later edition management.

No generic assignment form in this repository is ready for signature. The
identity and authority of the signing parties, governing law, consideration,
scope of assigned rights, treatment of moral rights, warranties, existing
material, withdrawal, credit, electronic signatures, and publisher
requirements require professional review in the applicable jurisdiction.
An assignment would take effect only after the final reviewed instrument is
executed by every required party; opening an issue, commit, or pull request is
not an assignment.

Until that process exists, the project will not merge pull requests that add,
translate, or materially rewrite Handbook expression. Readers may still open
issues that identify errors, sources, terminology, counterexamples, or
suggested improvements without pasting a substantial proposed passage. A
maintainer may independently investigate and draft a correction. Code and
package Documentation contributions continue under their separate terms.

This prospective policy does not retroactively assign copyright in any
existing Handbook text, translation, figure, or catalog entry. Unconfirmed
existing provenance remains pending and must be resolved before release.
