This is the controlling source gate for subvector or component efficiency.
The family asks how much a declared subset of resources or services can
improve when management does not have the same adjustment rights over every
quantity. It remains a canonical but non-executable family in the current
release.

## 1. Evidence boundary

The current broad primary anchor is Rolf Färe, Shawna Grosskopf, and C. A.
Knox Lovell (1994), *Production Frontiers*.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-94-015-7721-2).

The complete source treatment has not been frozen chapter-by-chapter and
equation-by-equation. In particular, the audited evidence does not yet fix:

1. the exact input- and output-subvector programmes;
2. returns to scale, disposability, score conventions, and score
   transformations;
3. whether non-adjustable quantities are equality commitments, one-sided
   bounds, or freely disposable residuals;
4. the source target and slack-completion programme; or
5. a complete primary numerical example or an exact independently compiled
   synthetic oracle suitable for source-equation validation.

No primary numerical example or exact independent synthetic oracle has been
completed. That missing executable validation is independently sufficient to
defer the family.

## 2. Why a zero-direction DDF is not the method

Setting selected components of a generic directional-distance vector to zero
only removes those quantities from the common first-stage movement path. It
does not establish a source-qualified subvector score or grant the zeroed
quantities a particular target interpretation.

For example, an input-only DDF with
$g_o^x=x_o\mathbin{\odot}1_S$ and $g_o^y=0$ conditionally yields
$X_S\lambda\leq(1-\beta)x_{o,S}$ and
$X_{\bar S}\lambda\leq x_{o,\bar S}$. This may permit compiler reuse if a
future primary-source audit proves that the subvector programme has exactly
that technology, with the transformation $\theta_S=1-\beta$. It does not
justify renaming the generic DDF:

- the generic DDF reports its own native score and efficiency transform;
- a zero direction does not imply an equality-fixed target; and
- generic slack completion may further change quantities whose direction
  component was zero.

Any future implementation may therefore compose a validated first-stage
kernel under explicit conditions, but it must retain source-specific score,
target, and method identities.

## 3. Non-equivalence boundary

Subvector/component distance must remain distinct from:

- Banker--Morey nondiscretionary-variable DEA, whose constraint and target
  semantics require a separate primary-source audit;
- short-run or quasi-fixed-factor models, where the time horizon, capital
  commitment, or state restriction may change the maintained technology;
- fitting unrestricted radial or directional DEA and inspecting one slack
  after optimization; and
- an arbitrary DDF created by setting some direction components to zero.

Shared language such as “fixed,” “component,” “energy-specific,” or
“non-controllable” does not establish mathematical or managerial
equivalence.

## 4. Gate for the next version

The family may be reopened only after:

1. the complete defining primary treatment is obtained and transcribed
   page-by-page for a narrowly named source leaf;
2. its orientation, adjustable subset, technology, returns to scale,
   disposability, native score, targets, slacks, and failure rules are frozen;
3. at least one complete primary-source numerical oracle is independently
   reproduced, or an exact synthetic oracle is certified by a separately
   written equation compiler;
4. any proposed DDF or radial composition is proved equation-by-equation,
   including the score transform and target account; and
5. counterexamples and tests distinguish the leaf from Banker--Morey,
   short-run/quasi-fixed models, unrestricted radial DEA, zero-direction DDF,
   and post-hoc slack inspection.

Until this gate is complete, no public class, machine method record,
executable documentation, or book recipe should be added.
