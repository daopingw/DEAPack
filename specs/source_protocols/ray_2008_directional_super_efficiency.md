The defining source is Subhash C. Ray, “The Directional Distance Function and
Measurement of Super-Efficiency: An Application to Airlines Data,” University
of Connecticut Working Paper 2004-16, subsequently published in the *Journal
of the Operational Research Society* (2008),
[DOI 10.1057/palgrave.jors.2602392](https://doi.org/10.1057/palgrave.jors.2602392).
The complete 15-page working paper contains the model, two numerical
applications, the interpretation, and the two limitations retained below.

## The management question

Ordinary DEA lets a frontier organization participate in the evidence used to
recognize that frontier. Ray asks a different counterfactual: after removing
organization $o$, how much simultaneous deterioration in its resource and
service record would the remaining organizations need before they could
replace it?

“Deterioration” has a precise source meaning. Resources may rise by one common
proportion while desirable services fall by the same proportion. A larger
reported value therefore means greater peer-replacement exposure under this
specific proportional operating account. It is not efficiency above 100
percent, a quality ranking, an outlier test, or evidence of a causal
management effect.

## Source technology and programme

For nonnegative inputs $x_j\in\mathbb R_+^m$ and desirable outputs
$y_j\in\mathbb R_+^s$, Ray's VRS production set is

$$
T=\left\{(x,y):
x\geq\sum_{j=1}^n\lambda_jx_j,
y\leq\sum_{j=1}^n\lambda_jy_j,
\sum_{j=1}^n\lambda_j=1,
\lambda_j\geq0
\right\}.
$$

The source directional distance uses the evaluated bundle itself as the
direction:

$$
D(x_o,y_o)=\max\left\{\beta:
\bigl((1-\beta)x_o,(1+\beta)y_o\bigr)\in T
\right\}.
$$

For super-efficiency, organization $o$ is removed. Let $T_{-o}$ be the VRS
technology generated by all otherwise eligible rows except $o$. Ray's
equation (8) is

$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{\lambda,\beta}\quad &\beta\\
\text{s.t.}\quad
&\sum_{j\ne o}\lambda_jy_j-\beta y_o\geq y_o,\\
&\sum_{j\ne o}\lambda_jx_j+\beta x_o\leq x_o,\\
&\sum_{j\ne o}\lambda_j=1,\\
&\lambda_j\geq0,\qquad\beta\text{ unrestricted}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The source directional boundary is

$$
x_o^{D}=(1-\beta_o)x_o,
\qquad
y_o^{D}=(1+\beta_o)y_o.
$$

The selected peer activity may leave input or output surplus relative to that
boundary. DEAPack records those feasibility gaps but does not add a second
slack-maximization problem: no such target-completion rule appears in Ray's
programme, and a solver-selected peer mixture need not be unique.

## Native score and sign contract

Ray calls $\beta_o$ the directional distance and reports the
Nerlove--Luenberger super-efficiency measure

$$
NL_o=1-\beta_o.
$$

The sign is economically important:

| $\beta_o$ | $NL_o$ | Reading |
|---:|---:|---|
| $>0$ | $<1$ | the organization remains ordinarily inefficient after exclusion |
| $=0$ | $=1$ | the remaining frontier reaches the observed operating record |
| $<0$ | $>1$ | peers require more resources and fewer services to replace the record |

Among source-super-efficient organizations, a more negative $\beta_o$ and a
larger $NL_o$ imply greater exposure. The result is a replacement-margin
ranking under the maintained sample and direction, not a percentage of
technical efficiency.

## Source limitations and fail-closed interpretation

Ray states two limitations rather than concealing them.

First, a focal input equal to zero can make the leave-one-out programme
infeasible because multiplying zero by $(1-\beta)$ can never reach the
strictly positive input used by every peer. The public source profile
therefore requires every focal input component to be strictly positive. It
does not add epsilon, translate data, change returns to scale, or substitute a
different super-efficiency model.

Second, if $\beta_o<-1$, then $NL_o>2$ and every positive desirable-output
component of $(1+\beta_o)y_o$ is negative. Ray retains the scalar for ranking
but calls the projection conceptually problematic. DEAPack consequently
preserves the certified raw `beta` and `nl_super_efficiency` values, marks
`source_projection_nonnegative=False`, and sets `score_valid=False`. Generic
plots and reports exclude that row from substantive score displays while its
diagnostic evidence remains auditable. The implementation does not clip the
score to two or present a negative-output boundary as a feasible management
target.

## Numerical evidence boundary

The source tables were inspected during method qualification, but their
observations and result vectors are not part of the public release. Public
regression evidence uses a project-created multivariate stress case and checks
equation structure and account reconstruction rather than a table
reproduction.

The paper's later empirical table supplies an important qualitative boundary:
an optimal scalar can imply an invalid projected quantity. The public release
does not redistribute that empirical table or its result vector. Instead,
`tests/test_ray_directional_super_efficiency.py` checks the same distinction on
an independently designed project stress case, together with sparse programme
dimensions, leave-one-out exclusion, account reconstruction, unit invariance,
and fail-closed certificates.

## Source-qualified implementation boundary

The public source profile fixes:

- VRS convex envelopment and ordinary free disposal;
- row-level self-exclusion from an otherwise eligible comparison population;
- strictly positive inputs, nonnegative desirable outputs with a positive row
  aggregate, and no undesirable outputs;
- the observed-bundle direction $(-x_o,y_o)$;
- one unrestricted signed $\beta$, the transform $NL=1-\beta$, the source
  directional boundary, peer activity, feasibility gaps, and diagnostic
  validity state; and
- deterministic full-frontier estimation without prices or statistical
  inference.

The source application is one global cross section. Contemporaneous, window,
sequential, or custom reference policies available through DEAPack are
transparent framework extensions: every focal row must still belong to its
base population before exact row exclusion, and every peer-only population
must remain nonempty.

CRS, NIRS, NDRS, arbitrary or range directions, input-only or output-only
directions, non-radial measures, undesirable-output technologies, automatic
zero repairs, modified feasibility models, group-level exclusion, network or
dynamic accounts, productivity indexes, uncertainty, and causal or welfare
interpretations are distinct methods. Ray's method composes the directional
distance idea with a fixed observed direction and a leave-one-out VRS
evaluation protocol; it is not an alias for generic DDF, Andersen--Petersen
radial super-efficiency, or super-SBM.
