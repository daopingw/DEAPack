# Core-family delivery matrix

**Status:** authoritative inverse audit of the frozen English handbook route,
3 August 2026.

This matrix prevents comprehensiveness from becoming a catalogue of paper
titles. It begins with the handbook's 18 reader questions and asks whether each
question has the software, independent evidence, technical Documentation, and
teaching treatment needed to support it. It does not begin with the public
method registry and try to place every implementation in the book.

The governing scope decision is in
[`MAINSTREAM_BOOK_SCOPE_AUDIT.md`](MAINSTREAM_BOOK_SCOPE_AUDIT.md). The exact
chapter route and admission test are in
[`BOOK_ARCHITECTURE.md`](BOOK_ARCHITECTURE.md). This matrix may identify work
inside a retained chapter, but it authorizes no additional chapter.

## Reading the status column

- **Closed** means the present book claim has a public implementation where one
  is needed, independent evidence, complete technical Documentation, and a
  tested teaching account.
- **Closed with bounded debt** means the core lesson is supported, while a
  public adjacent policy, orientation, or generalized implementation still
  needs stronger independent validation. The debt cannot be used to broaden
  the book's claim.
- **Partially closed** means the chapter teaches a recognized core family but
  an economically distinct, mainstream route within that same family remains
  behind the source gate.
- **Conceptual foundation** means the chapter defines the study contract rather
  than estimating a separate model. Requiring a new estimator merely to make
  such a chapter look computational would be a category error.

“Independent evidence” means an analytical certificate, published numerical
reproduction, or separately compiled source-form oracle. Agreement with the
production implementation's own output is not independent evidence.

## Explicit non-gaps

The following remain package Documentation, registry, test, or later-version
subjects. They must not reappear in a future “coverage gap” list merely because
they have names in the literature:

- paper-specific directions, weights, normalizations, windows, rankings, and
  decompositions;
- multiplicative, range-directional, generalized-distance, BAM, and
  non-separable hybrid measures already assigned to a core parent lesson;
- cross-efficiency and super-efficiency until their mainstream source and
  independent-oracle gates close;
- MPSS, physical capacity, Färe--Primont, conditional frontiers, and full
  inference until the evidence requirements in the scope audit close;
- sequential, environmental, dynamic-network, multiperiod-aggregation,
  metafrontier--SBM, and other intersections or technical protocols; and
- application-specific energy, material, treatment, allocation, industry, or
  policy accounts.

The registry may remain much larger than the book. That asymmetry is a design
success: the package preserves reproducible technical work while the handbook
teaches the field's durable economic and managerial ideas.
