# Analytical certificate for the Green--Cook radial FCH

**Method ID:** `static.radial.fch.green_cook_2004`  
**Validation kind:** `analytically_derived`  
**Published reproduction:** no  
**Production compiler reused:** no

This protocol certifies the public input- and output-oriented radial
free-coordination-hull account on one exact four-organization fixture. It is
not a reproduction of a numerical table in Green and Cook (2004). The source
defines the maintained binary-subset technology; every numerical expectation
below is derived independently with rational arithmetic and exhaustive
enumeration of the fixture's nonempty coalitions.

## Maintained technology

Let $S$ be a nonempty subset of the $n$ observed organizations. Its coordinated
activity is the componentwise sum

$$
x(S)=\sum_{j\in S}x_j,
\qquad
y(S)=\sum_{j\in S}y_j.
$$

This is exactly the activity generated by a binary vector
$z\in\{0,1\}^n$ with $\mathbf 1^\top z\geq1$. Each observed organization can
enter once, distinct organizations can be coordinated, and neither fractional
division nor repeated copies are admitted. Ordinary free disposal completes
the technology.

For a strictly positive evaluated activity $(x_o,y_o)$, a subset $S$ is
input-feasible when $y(S)\geq y_o$. Conditional on that subset, its smallest
input contraction factor is

$$
\theta_o(S)=\max_i\frac{x_i(S)}{x_{io}}.
$$

The FCH input score is therefore

$$
\theta_o^{FCH}
=
\min_{\varnothing\ne S\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}:\,y(S)\geq y_o}
\theta_o(S).
$$

For output orientation, $S$ is feasible when $x(S)\leq x_o$, and its largest
supported output expansion is

$$
\phi_o(S)=\min_r\frac{y_r(S)}{y_{ro}}.
$$

Thus

$$
\phi_o^{FCH}
=
\max_{\varnothing\ne S\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}:\,x(S)\leq x_o}
\phi_o(S).
$$

The public harmonized efficiency is $\theta_o^{FCH}$ under input orientation
and $1/\phi_o^{FCH}$ under output orientation.

These formulae provide a finite exact certificate. There are precisely
$2^n-1$ nonempty binary subsets. Evaluating every one with integer sums and
exact rational ratios proves the global minimum or maximum without a
mixed-integer solver, an LP relaxation, a production compiler, or an assumed
incumbent bound.

## Exact four-organization fixture

The fixture has one strictly positive additive resource and one strictly
positive desirable service:

| Organization | resource $x$ | service $y$ |
|---|---:|---:|
| A | 3 | 6 |
| B | 4 | 5 |
| C | 12 | 14 |
| E | 10 | 10 |

With four observations, the oracle visits all $2^4-1=15$ nonempty subsets for
every appraisal. The following input account is obtained by exact comparison
of every output-feasible subset:

| Evaluated organization | feasible subsets | $\theta$ | unique optimum | radial activity | reference activity | $(s_x^-,s_y^+)$ |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|---|
| A | 14 | $1$ | A | $(3,6)$ | $(3,6)$ | $(0,0)$ |
| B | 15 | $3/4$ | A | $(3,5)$ | $(3,6)$ | $(0,1)$ |
| C | 11 | $1$ | C | $(12,14)$ | $(12,14)$ | $(0,0)$ |
| E | 13 | $7/10$ | A+B | $(7,10)$ | $(7,11)$ | $(0,1)$ |

The output account follows from exact comparison of every input-feasible
subset:

| Evaluated organization | feasible subsets | $\phi$ | harmonized efficiency | unique optimum | radial activity | reference activity | $(s_x^-,s_y^+)$ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|---|---|
| A | 1 | $1$ | $1$ | A | $(3,6)$ | $(3,6)$ | $(0,0)$ |
| B | 2 | $6/5$ | $5/6$ | A | $(4,6)$ | $(3,6)$ | $(1,0)$ |
| C | 5 | $1$ | $1$ | C | $(12,14)$ | $(12,14)$ | $(0,0)$ |
| E | 4 | $11/10$ | $10/11$ | A+B | $(10,11)$ | $(7,11)$ | $(3,0)$ |

Here the radial activity is $(\theta x_o,y_o)$ for input orientation and
$(x_o,\phi y_o)$ for output orientation. The reference activity is
$(x(S),y(S))$ for the unique optimal subset. The free-disposal residuals are
$s_x^-=\theta x_o-x(S)$ and $s_y^+=y(S)-y_o$ in the input account, and
$s_x^-=x_o-x(S)$ and $s_y^+=y(S)-\phi y_o$ in the output account.

The unique optimum in each row means that successful fixed-score completion
cannot change the coalition on this fixture. It does not imply that FCH
coalitions or targets are unique in general.

## Executable protocol and independence boundary

`tests/test_fch_independent_oracle.py` implements the following protocol:

1. construct all nonempty subsets with `itertools.combinations`;
2. sum their inputs and outputs with Python integers;
3. screen feasibility component by component;
4. compute every candidate ratio with `fractions.Fraction`;
5. take the exact finite minimum or maximum and retain all tied subsets; and
6. compare the resulting scores, reciprocal output efficiencies, unique
   coalitions, radial activities, reference activities, and disposal residuals
   with the public `FCH` result.

The derivation functions are guarded against dependencies on DEAPack, FCH,
NumPy, SciPy, `linprog`, `milp`, or any solver. The public API is invoked only
after the exact expectations have been produced by the independent enumerator.

## Claim boundary

This analytical certificate is deliberately narrow. It establishes both
radial orientations for all four observations in this one-input,
one-desirable-output, strictly positive, cross-sectional, self-inclusive
fixture under the full eligible comparison population. It establishes the
exact native scores, harmonized efficiencies, feasible-subset counts, unique
fixture-specific optimal coalitions, and the associated completed radial and
reference activities and free-disposal residuals.

It does **not** claim:

- a published Green--Cook numerical reproduction or an independent package
  cross-implementation;
- coalition or target uniqueness outside the named fixture;
- a certificate for external reference sets, zeros, negative quantities,
  undesirable outputs, panels, multiple input/output dimensions, inference,
  or robustness analysis;
- a numerical guarantee for arbitrary mixed-integer problems; or
- any score, nesting, or equivalence claim for FDH, FRH, CCR, or VRS.

Those broader properties remain supported, if at all, by their separately
named sources and tests. The defining source for the maintained FCH technology
is Green and Cook (2004), DOI `10.1057/palgrave.jors.2601773`.
